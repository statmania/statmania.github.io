About These Curves

  • Love Equation — a heart traced from a single sinusoidal parametrization; the classic.
  • Butterfly Curve — discovered by Temple Fay, it needs a full $24\pi$ sweep to complete its wings.
  • Rose Curve — the polar equation $r=\cos(5\theta)$ draws a 5-petaled flower.
  • Astroid — the path traced by a point on a small circle rolling inside a circle 4× its size.
  • Lissajous Figure — two perpendicular sine waves at a 3:2 frequency ratio, the pattern behind oscilloscope art.
  • Deltoid — a hypocycloid with 3 cusps, traced by a circle rolling inside one 3× its size.
  • Drag the Points slider to trade off smoothness vs. performance, and switch Drawing Mode to see the individual sampled points instead of connecting lines.
