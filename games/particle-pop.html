How to Play

  • Particles of 20 different sizes drift around, randomly changing direction every second or two.
  • Click one to pop it and score points — smaller particles are worth more (1–20 points), but they're also faster and harder to hit.
  • Every particle has a lifespan and will fade away on its own if you don't catch it in time — no points either way, it's just gone.
  • Clicking empty space scores 0 points but still counts as a click. Every click could have earned 20 points, so Target Score = clicks × 20 and your Achievement Index = Achieved Score ÷ Target Score.
  • Your top 10 scores for each time limit are saved in this browser.
  • A new particle spawns for every one that's popped or fades, so the board stays full until time runs out.
