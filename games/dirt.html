How to Play

  • The target drifts around the board, curving off in a new direction every second or two — track it and click (or tap) it to throw a clump of dirt.
  • Each throw scores based on which layer it lands in — deeper layers, closer to the core, are worth more.
  • Land outside every ring and it's a Miss — no points, and your streak resets.
  • Chain hits together to build a streak; every 5 in a row adds a small score multiplier.
  • Score as much as you can before the timer runs out, then hit New Game to try again.
