How to Play

  • Move the crosshair to where you want the arrow to land (the bow compensates for the arc), press and hold to draw the bow (the power ring fills — a fuller draw means a faster, flatter arrow), and release to shoot. On a phone, touch, hold and lift.
  • The arrow takes time to arrive, so lead the target: the target keeps sliding up and down while the arrow is in the air, so aim where it will be, not where it is. A faster (fuller) draw means less lead.
  • The rings are worth 1–10 points, with the center worth 10. A miss scores 0. Only one arrow can be in flight at a time.
  • The target speeds up every 3 arrows and starts to wobble.
  • Every arrow could have earned 10 points, so Target Score = shots × 10 and Achievement Index = Achieved Score ÷ Target Score.
  • Need a break? Press Pause (or the space bar). The round also pauses if you switch tabs. Your top 10 for each arrow count is saved in this browser.
