Scoring & Levels

  • Correct pick: 10 points + 5 per second left on the clock.
  • Wrong pick or timeout: one mistake, no points.
  • Rounds 1–3: the longest word beats the rest by 3+ letters; 4–7: by 2+; 8+: by just 1.
  • Words get longer, more of them appear (up to 8) and the time shrinks (down to 3.5s).

How to Play

  • Each round, several words appear at random spots inside the box and stay until the visible timer runs out.
  • Click the longest word (most letters). The next round starts the moment you pick, and the result of the last round shows under the box.
  • A wrong pick, or letting the timer reach zero, is a mistake. Make 5 mistakes and the game ends.
  • Need a break? Press Pause (or the space bar) — the words hide and the timer freezes. The game also pauses if you switch tabs.
  • Your top 10 scores are saved in this browser.
