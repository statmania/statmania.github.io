How to Play

  • You're shown a Current card with a real value, and a Next card with the value hidden.
  • Guess whether the Next value is Higher or Lower than the Current one.
  • Guess right and the Next card becomes the new Current card — your streak keeps growing.
  • Guess wrong and the round ends; your best streak is saved so you can try to beat it.
  • All figures are real-world approximations meant for play, not citation — pick a category and see how far you can go.
