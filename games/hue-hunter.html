How to Play

  • Choose the color you'll hunt (or pick ? for a random one), then press Start or click the board. Balls of six colors and 20 sizes drift around.
  • Pop a ball of your color to score its size value — smaller balls are worth more (1–20 points), but they're faster and harder to hit.
  • Pop a ball of any other color and you lose half its points (rounded up). Think before you click — a tiny wrong ball still hurts.
  • Clicking empty space scores 0 points but still counts as a click. Every click could have earned 20 points, so Target Score = clicks × 20 and Achievement Index = Achieved Score ÷ Target Score (shown as 0% if your score is negative).
  • Balls fade away on their own after a few seconds, and new ones keep arriving, with about a third of them in your color.
  • Need a break? Press Pause (or the space bar) — the timer and balls freeze, and the round also pauses if you switch tabs.
  • Your top 10 scores for each time limit are saved in this browser.
