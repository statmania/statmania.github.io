Play X vs O against a smart AI (Minimax algorithm)

Tic-Tac-Toe (You vs Computer)

You are X. Computer is O.

Game Rules
  1. Click on any empty cell to make your move. Your moves are X.
  2. After your move, the computer will play as O using the Minimax algorithm (perfect play).
  3. Get 3 X in a row, column, or diagonal to win.
  4. The computer will also try to get 3 O to win.
  5. If the board fills with no winner, it's a draw.
