Scoring & Levels

  • Correct pick: 10 points + 5 per second left on the clock.
  • Wrong pick or timeout: one mistake, no points.
  • Rounds 1–4: numbers up to 99; 5+: up to 999 (never more than 3 digits).
  • Each round adds numbers (up to 12) and trims the time (down to 2.2s).

Top 10

How to Play

  • Each round, several numbers appear at random spots inside the box and stay until the visible timer runs out.
  • Click the highest number. The next round starts the moment you pick, and the result of the last round shows under the box.
  • A wrong pick, or letting the timer reach zero, is a mistake. Make 5 mistakes and the game ends.
  • Need a break? Press Pause (or the space bar) — the numbers hide and the timer freezes. The game also pauses if you switch tabs.
  • Your top 10 scores are saved in this browser.
